Introduction:

To make the traditional Romanian dish Sarmale, you will need the following ingredients

Ingredients:

  • head of cabbage
  • 500 g ground meat
  • ½ cup rice
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cups water or broth
  • Sour cream, for serving

Sarmale is a traditional Romanian dish made with cabbage leaves stuffed with seasoned meat and rice.

Sarmale

  1. 1- Prepare the cabbage:

    Separate the cabbage leaves carefully and soften them in hot water. Let them cool.

  2. 2- Prepare the filling:

    Heat the oil in a pan and cook the chopped onion until soft. Add the rice and tomato paste, then mix wel

  3. 3- Add the meat:

    Put the ground meat in a bowl and mix it with the onion, rice, tomato paste, salt, and black peppe

  4. 4- Fill the leaves:

    Place a small amount of filling in the center of each cabbage leaf and roll it tightly.

  5. 5- Arrange the sarmale:

    Place the cabbage rolls in a large pot and add the bay leaves.

  6. 6- Add the liquid:

    Pour in enough water or broth to almost cover the rolls.

  7. 7- Cook slowly:

    Cover the pot and cook over low heat for about 2 hours, until the cabbage and filling are tender.
