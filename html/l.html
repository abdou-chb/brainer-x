MIKE A MICI

Introduction

Mici, also known as Mititei, are one of Romania’s most iconic traditional dishes. These small, skinless grilled meat rolls are made from a carefully seasoned mixture of minced meat, garlic, and aromatic spices. Traditionally cooked over a hot charcoal grill, mici develop a beautifully browned exterior while remaining juicy and tender inside. They are commonly served with mustard, fresh bread, and a cold drink, making them a popular choice for Romanian family gatherings and outdoor meals.

componenets

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 200 g minced lamb or another 200 g beef
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, finely crushed
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • ½ teaspoon ground coriander
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • 100–150 ml cold sparkling water
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, for grilling
